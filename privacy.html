Privacy

What we keep, and why

Last updated 25 October 2026

Playing without signing in

You can play every character without an account. Nothing about those games leaves your browser. A game in progress is kept in your browser's session storage so a reload doesn't lose it, and your sound setting is kept in local storage.

Signing in with Google

Online play and your profile need a Google sign-in. Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture. We show your name and picture to the players you meet. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

What we store

For each signed-in player we store: your name and picture from Google, the moves of every game you play (online and against the characters), the notes you write in match chat, and a short summary of your play style that other players can see on your profile.

Your opponents see the moves and chat of the matches they played with you. Nobody else sees your games. Other players can see only your name, picture, number of games, cooperation rate and closest characters.

Where it is stored

The data is kept in a database run by Supabase. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything except running the game. There are no ads and no tracking scripts.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time. See Delete your account.
